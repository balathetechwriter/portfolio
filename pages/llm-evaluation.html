Evaluation.

Evaluating AI-generated responses for factual accuracy, relevance, completeness, coherence, instruction following, hallucinations, safety, and policy compliance.

Evaluation practice

Human judgment, made consistent.

Worked across AI data annotation, model training, language evaluation, human preference evaluation, RLHF-related feedback workflows, and training-data quality validation.

  • Compared multiple AI responses and ranked outputs using defined preference guidelines.
  • Identified hallucinations, contradictions, unsupported claims, and factual errors.
  • Evaluated instruction following against prompts and task requirements.
  • Reviewed AI-generated content for safety and policy compliance.
  • Performed structured response scoring and maintained consistent evaluation decisions.

RLHF & preference evaluation

Performed comparative evaluations to identify relative strengths and weaknesses, ranked candidate responses against factuality, relevance, completeness, and adherence-to-instruction criteria, and applied human-feedback principles to identify preferred output characteristics.
